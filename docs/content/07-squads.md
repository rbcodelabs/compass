# Squads

Squads let you organise objects by team so that each squad can focus on their slice of the product work without noise from other teams.

## Creating Squads

Go to **Settings → Squads** to create and manage squads for your workspace. Each squad needs a name (e.g. "Growth", "Platform", "Mobile"). Squads are workspace-scoped — all workspace members see all squads.

## Assigning Squads to Objects

Almost every object type in Compass supports squad assignment:

- **Objectives** — Assign during creation or in the objective detail panel
- **Opportunities** — Assign on the discovery card or in the opportunity detail panel
- **Experiments** — Assign in the experiment detail panel
- **Roadmap Items** — Assign during creation or in the roadmap item detail

When you assign an object to a squad, it appears in that squad's filtered view.

## Filtering by Squad

Every section page (OKRs, Discovery, Experiments, Roadmap, Feedback) shows a **squad filter bar** at the top. Click a squad name to filter the page to only show objects assigned to that squad. Click again to deselect. Filters stack — you can select multiple squads at once.

The filter selection persists for the duration of your session but resets on reload. Squad filters affect only what you see — they do not change the underlying data.
