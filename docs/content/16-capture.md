# Capture

Capture is the starting point for customer feedback, ideas, and research. It keeps raw input connected to the opportunities and decisions it informs.

## Studies

Open **Capture** and choose **New study** to create a customer interview. Give the study a clear learning goal and enter one open-ended discussion question per line.

After creating the study, Compass shows a secure participant link. Share that link with participants so they can complete the interview without a Compass account. Links expire after 30 days and Compass stores only a secure hash, so save the displayed link when it is created. You can generate a replacement link from the study page; doing so invalidates the old one.

Completed sessions and transcripts remain attached to the study and its workspace.

## Inbox

The Capture **Inbox** opens the existing feedback workflow for ideas and bugs. Customer portal submissions, votes, attachments, opportunity links, and roadmap actions continue to work as before.

## Privacy and review

Participant pages explain that responses are shared with the research team. Research links grant access only to their associated study. AI-generated findings will require review before they become Compass evidence or affect discovery objects.
